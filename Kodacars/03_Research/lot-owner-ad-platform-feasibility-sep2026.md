# Lot Owner Acquisition — Ad Platform Feasibility (X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Meta)

> Date: 08 September 2026
> Prepared by: Rishabh B | Damco Digital
> Purpose: Confirm whether the Lot Owner target audience (hotel GMs, airport parking lot owners/operators, facilities decision-makers — B2B, nationwide USA) is present and precisely targetable on X, YouTube, LinkedIn, and Meta, ahead of expanding KodaCars' paid social mix.

---

## Target Audience Definition

Professional lot/facility operators — hotel GMs, airport parking lot owners, facilities decision-makers. Seniority: CXO, Director, Manager, VP, Owner. Industries: Hospitality, Facilities Management, Real Estate, Commercial Parking Operations. Geography: Nationwide USA. This is the supply-side B2B partner audience — separate from the consumer/traveler demand-side audience.

---

## Platform-by-Platform Findings

### LinkedIn — Strong fit (baseline, already validated)
- Real seniority × industry firmographic targeting via Campaign Manager — matches this TG almost exactly.
- Addressable audience: 1.2M+ | CPM: ~$3.43 | CTR: ~2.0%
- $1,200/30-day plan → ~349,854 impressions / ~85,335 unique reach / ~6,997 clicks (per existing LinkedIn Awareness Campaign proposal).
- **Primary channel for this TG.**

### Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — Medium-Strong fit (corrected — see note below)
- Meta's 2021–2023 detailed-targeting cleanup removed ~5,000 *sensitive-category* options (health, sexual orientation, religion, politics) — it did **not** remove general job-title/employer/industry targeting.
- Meta's stricter "Special Ad Category" restrictions (no age/gender/zip targeting, no exclusions) only apply to ads literally offering employment, housing, or credit. A lot-owner partnership outreach campaign is not a job/housing/credit ad, so it is never forced into that restricted bucket and retains full detailed targeting.
- Demographics → Work (job title, employer, industry) combined with Interest targeting (hotel, motel, real estate) is a real, buildable audience — this is what a prior Damco campaign build verified directly in Meta Ads Manager at **1.8M–2.2M**.
- Caveat: Meta's job-title/employer data is inferred from profile fields, page follows, and group activity — not a verified professional record like LinkedIn's. Expect more dilution/noise in delivery than LinkedIn's tighter seniority+industry filter, even though the reachable pool is larger.
- CPM (US, 2026): general B2B ~$8–22, Lead Gen objective ~$25–40.
- **Verdict: viable secondary scale channel — use for incremental reach/frequency alongside LinkedIn, not as an equally precise substitute.**

### Google Search (keyword intent, not YouTube/Display) — Medium fit
- No firmographic targeting exists on Google Ads, but exact-match high-intent keywords ("monetize my parking lot," "airport parking lease partner") self-select toward genuine decision-makers by search intent.
- Likely low search volume for such niche terms — treat as a small, always-on demand-capture campaign, not a scale channel.
- CPC ~$0.50–3.50.

### YouTube / Google Display — Weak-Medium fit
- No job-title/seniority/employer targeting layer on Google Ads (Custom Intent/Affinity merged into Custom Segments, still keyword/URL-based, not people-based).
- Custom Segments (keyword/URL) + In-Market/Affinity layering gives reach but not confirmed-buyer precision — expect real wasted spend if used for cold acquisition.
- CPM ~$16–21, CPV ~$0.03–0.12.
- **Best use: retargeting layer** (site visitors, LinkedIn/Meta ad clickers) once a pool exists to build Custom Audiences from — not a cold-targeting channel.

### X (Twitter) — Weakest / Not recommended
- No professional/firmographic targeting layer exists on X at all — no job title, seniority, employer, or industry filter.
- Only proxies available: keyword targeting, follower-lookalike (of a chosen handle), or an uploaded ABM contact list.
- CPM ~$10–20+ for narrow B2B, CPC ~$1.50–4 — pricier than LinkedIn for materially worse precision.
- **Not recommended as a cold-acquisition channel** for this TG; only viable as a small supplementary channel if KodaCars already has a contact list to upload.

## Additional Platforms (Round 2 — Quora, Reddit, Microsoft/Bing Ads)

### Microsoft Advertising (Bing/MSN/Outlook/Edge) — LinkedIn Profile Targeting — Medium-Strong fit
- Microsoft owns LinkedIn and offers "LinkedIn profile targeting" on Search, Audience (Native), and Performance Max campaigns — confirmed live as of mid-2026.
- Targeting dimensions: Company, Industry, Job function (job title is not a dimension). Job seniority and company size are rolling out in some accounts as a newer, phased feature — verify availability in our own account before planning around it.
- Important limit: this is a **bid-only** modifier, not a hard filter like native LinkedIn Campaign Manager. It adjusts bids up for matching profiles rather than restricting who sees the ad — meaningfully less precise than LinkedIn itself.
- Reach: 200M+ LinkedIn profiles matchable in the US. Reported (vendor-sourced, unverified in a live account) cost impact: ~8-12% CPC increase when adding LinkedIn targeting, ~47% lift in conversion rate, ~31% lower blended CPA than running LinkedIn directly.
- Additive, not redundant: reaches LinkedIn members while on Bing (search intent) or MSN/Outlook/Edge (display) — different inventory and context from native LinkedIn.
- **Verdict: best-performing of the round-2 platforms.** Recommend pairing with the same high-intent Search keywords as the Google Search test, using LinkedIn industry/job-function as a bid booster rather than running it as a standalone audience buy.

### Quora Ads — Weak-Medium fit
- Targeting: topic targeting, question targeting, keyword targeting, interest/behavioral, custom/lookalike audiences. No job-title or firmographic layer — Quora's own materials frame topic/question targeting as the substitute for job-title filtering, not an equivalent.
- Reach mechanism: target topics/questions like "commercial parking management," "hotel revenue management," "monetize a parking lot." Works on intent, not confirmed professional identity — a curious consumer or student could land in the same topic cluster as a hotel GM.
- Cost/scale: no published CPM/CPC benchmark for this niche. Quora's platform minimum is $5/day per campaign, but that floor is not enough to gather real data — a statistically useful test typically needs $50-200/day per audience over a 2-3 week window. Audience size likely small (thin niche topic clusters, not millions).
- **Verdict: only worth testing if we're prepared to fund a real $50-200/day, 2-3 week test — not a $5/day trial. At that budget it's still a small exploratory spend relative to LinkedIn/Meta, but it is not free to find out.**

### Reddit Ads — Weak fit
- Targeting: subreddit/community targeting (5,000+ subscriber minimum), interest targeting, keyword targeting, custom audiences/lookalikes. No job-title or firmographic layer at all — same structural gap as X.
- Relevant communities: r/CommercialRealEstate and r/PropertyManagement touch adjacent topics but skew toward investors/landlords/small operators, not senior hotel GMs or airport lot executives. No dense, decision-maker-heavy community exists for hospitality/parking/facilities the way it does for SaaS/tech (r/SaaS, r/startups).
- Cost: general CPM $5-25, CPC $0.20-4.00; favorable B2B CPA benchmarks cited elsewhere ($50-100) are tied to platforms with dense tech/SaaS communities — that density doesn't carry over to this vertical.
- **Verdict: not recommended.** Same missing-firmographic-layer problem as X, without even a strong community fallback.

### Other options flagged (not deep-researched — for later-stage consideration)
- **Demandbase / 6sense (ABM display):** Real company-level firmographic + intent targeting, but enterprise-priced (~$30K+/year commitments) — overkill for KodaCars' current acquisition volume. Revisit at scale.
- **Trade publication programmatic** (Hotel Business, Hotel News Now, Parking Today, IPMI/NPA media): Reaches an already-qualified, self-selected professional readership with no ad-targeting guesswork — often cheaper and more precisely matched than social platforms, but slower to set up (media-kit/IO based, not self-serve). Worth a follow-up look.
- **G2/Capterra-style intent platforms:** Not a fit — built for software-buyer intent, not physical lot-owner/facilities decision-makers.
- **Nextdoor:** Only relevant for hyper-local individual small-lot/motel owners in specific test metros, not nationwide corporate decision-makers. Not a priority.

## Final Combined Ranking

| Tier | Platform | Fit | Role in Media Mix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | **LinkedIn** | Strong | Lead channel — precise firmographic targeting, proven CPM/CTR |
| Secondary | **Meta** | Medium-Strong | Scale channel — larger reach (1.8-2.2M), less precise than LinkedIn |
| Secondary/Test | **Microsoft Advertising (LinkedIn Profile Targeting)** | Medium-Strong | Layer LinkedIn industry/job-function bid-boost on top of Search keywords; incremental reach beyond native LinkedIn inventory |
| Small always-on test | **Google Search** | Medium | High-intent keyword self-selection |
| Small exploratory test | **Quora** | Weak-Medium | Topic/question-targeted test; needs a real $50-200/day, 2-3 week budget to get useful data (the $5/day platform minimum is not a real test), deprioritize if results are thin |
| Retargeting-only | **YouTube / Google Display** | Weak-Medium | Retarget site visitors / LinkedIn-Meta ad clickers only, not cold acquisition |
| Not recommended | **X (Twitter)** | Weakest | No firmographic layer; costs more than LinkedIn for worse precision |
| Not recommended | **Reddit** | Weak | No firmographic layer and no dense decision-maker community for this vertical |

## Recommendation

Run LinkedIn as the primary lot-owner acquisition channel and Meta as a secondary scale channel (Work demographics + hospitality/real-estate interest targeting, already verified at 1.8-2.2M reach). Add Microsoft Advertising's LinkedIn Profile Targeting as a new secondary/test channel, paired with the same high-intent keywords as the Google Search test, using LinkedIn industry/job-function as a bid modifier rather than a standalone buy. Run Google Search and Quora as small, low-cost always-on/exploratory tests for genuine intent capture. Hold YouTube/Display for retargeting once a warm pool exists. Do not add X or Reddit for this TG — neither has a firmographic targeting mechanism, and neither has the community/cost advantage that might otherwise offset that gap.

## Correction Note

An earlier pass of this research incorrectly concluded Meta's job-title/employer targeting had been effectively dismantled industry-wide. That conflated Meta's 2021–2023 sensitive-category cleanup and Special Ad Category restrictions (which apply only to actual job/housing/credit ads) with general B2B targeting availability. Corrected after cross-checking against a prior Damco campaign build that verified a 1.8M–2.2M audience for this exact TG directly in Meta Ads Manager.
