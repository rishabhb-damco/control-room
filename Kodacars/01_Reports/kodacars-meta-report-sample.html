Keep boosting the P4 Trust posts consistently. Trust content (gated lots, insurance, telematics) has lower CTR but high saves — operators are bookmarking it for later. This is the content that converts warm audiences in Phase 2.

04  ·  Measurement
Key observations this month
Organic posts are doing the groundwork — boosting amplifies what already works. The strategy is working as designed: organic posts build the page and signal quality to Meta’s algorithm. The top 5 performers are then pushed to 28K–45K verified operators at a CPM of $10–$14, a fraction of what traditional B2B lead gen costs.
Frequency is capped at 2–3 times per week per operator — by design. This ensures KodaCars builds familiarity without fatigue. The goal is not immediate action, but name recognition. When sales outreach begins, operators will already know who KodaCars is.
Watch: Meta B2B targeting is broad by nature — LinkedIn is not. The audience is based on interest and job-title signals, not verified employment data. This means some impressions will fall outside the ideal profile. The 1.8M–2.2M audience pool is built and verified in Meta Ads Manager to minimise this, but it is an inherent characteristic of Meta B2B targeting at this budget level.
