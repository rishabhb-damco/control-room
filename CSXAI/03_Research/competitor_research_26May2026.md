## Part 2 — Platform Strategy

### The Question
CSXAI has zero existing database. The goal: identify the best channel(s) to generate qualified leads from financial institutions and real estate companies in the US.

---

### Platform 1: LinkedIn Ads

**Verdict: Recommended — Phase 1 for financial institutions**

#### Why It Works for CSXAI
- LinkedIn is the only platform where you can target by **job function + seniority + industry** with high accuracy
- Can reach: VP Operations, Director of Customer Service, CTO, COO, Head of Technology at banks (200–1,000 employee companies = community banks)
- For real estate institutions: can target Brokerage Owners, Directors of Operations, VP Technology at real estate firms
- LinkedIn delivers **113% average ROAS for B2B SaaS** (outperforms Google and Meta)
- **Thought Leader Ads** — the most effective format in 2026 — promote posts from Shaivi/leadership and get 1.7x higher CTR than standard ads. This is naturally aligned with CSXAI's LinkedIn organic plan (8–12 posts/month starting June)

#### Recommended Targeting Setup (Financial Institutions)
| Layer | Value |
|---|---|
| Job Function | Operations, Information Technology, Finance |
| Seniority | Director, VP, CXO |
| Industry | Banking, Financial Services |
| Company Size | 200–2,000 employees |
| Geography | United States |
| Exclude | Students, Entry-level |

#### Recommended Targeting Setup (Real Estate)
| Layer | Value |
|---|---|
| Job Function | Operations, Information Technology, Business Development |
| Seniority | Director, VP, CXO, Owner |
| Industry | Real Estate |
| Company Size | 50–1,000 employees |
| Geography | United States |

#### Best Ad Formats
1. **Thought Leader Ads** — promote posts from CSXAI leadership/Shaivi. 4–6x higher CTR. Best for awareness + reach.
2. **Lead Gen Forms** — native LinkedIn form for demo requests. Reduces friction. Best for conversion.
3. **Document Ads** — promote the lead magnet (when ready). Best for list building.

#### Cost Reality
- LinkedIn CPL for B2B SaaS: typically $80–$250 per lead
- Minimum recommended monthly budget: $3,000–$5,000 to gather enough data
- **Recommendation: Start with organic + Thought Leader Ads (low cost), scale with Lead Gen Forms once content is validated**

---

### Platform 2: Google Ads

**Verdict: Recommended — Phase 2 or parallel (intent-based search)**

#### Why It Works
- Captures decision-makers **actively searching** for solutions
- High-intent keywords: "AI voice agent for banks," "automate customer service calls banking," "24/7 voice AI financial services"
- Complements LinkedIn (LinkedIn = push/awareness; Google = pull/intent)
- B2B SaaS Google Ads reward intent-tier structuring over volume

#### Audience Availability Issue
- Google doesn't offer job title targeting like LinkedIn
- Audience is smaller and harder to find vs. LinkedIn
- But searchers are at **higher buying intent** — better CPL-to-conversion ratio

#### Recommended Keyword Clusters
| Cluster | Example Keywords | Intent |
|---|---|---|
| Direct solution search | "AI voice agent for banks," "voice AI financial services" | High |
| Problem-aware | "automate customer service calls banking," "reduce call centre costs bank" | Medium-High |
| Competitor search | "Eltropy alternative," "Glia alternative" | High |
| Real estate specific | "AI voice agent real estate," "automate property inquiry calls" | High |

#### Cost Reality
- CPL for B2B SaaS on Google: $50–$150 (lower than LinkedIn, but lower quality)
- **Recommendation: Run Google alongside LinkedIn once content and landing pages are validated. Start with exact + phrase match on high-intent terms only.**

---

### Platform 3: Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram)

**Verdict: Not recommended for financial institutions. Limited value for real estate institutions.**

#### Why It Doesn't Work Well for CSXAI's ICP
- Meta cannot target by job title/function with the precision LinkedIn offers
- Financial institution decision-makers (VP Operations, CTO at a community bank) are not reachable reliably on Meta
- Meta's B2B targeting relies on interest + behaviour layers — not company or role
- For **real estate individual agents** (SMB), Meta works; for **real estate institutions** (CSXAI's target), it does not

#### One Possible Exception
Meta could work for **retargeting** — running ads to people who visited csxintelligence.com. Low budget, low effort, low risk. Add pixel now, retarget later.
