## Key Observations

1. **`eltropy` at 1,900/mo is the biggest keyword in the entire set.** More people research Eltropy every month than search for any generic AI voice term. Competitor conquesting here is the highest-ROI opportunity in this keyword universe — low competition (index: 3), reasonable CPCs ($5–$35), and buyers are already in active vendor evaluation mode.

2. **Conversational AI banking is exploding.** `conversational ai in banking` +400% and `conversational ai banking` +300% in 3 months. CPCs are near zero now. This is the time to get in before it becomes competitive.

3. **The real estate keyword space is a trap.** 1,900/mo searches for "real estate virtual assistant" sounds attractive but is entirely dominated by VA staffing. Bidding here wastes budget on realtors looking to hire a $15/hr Filipino VA, not institutions evaluating AI platforms.

4. **`kasisto competitors` has High competition despite 10/mo volume.** Someone specifically searching "kasisto competitors" is deep in vendor evaluation. The High competition flag means other vendors (potentially CSXAI's competitors) are already bidding on it. CSXAI should too.

5. **`conversational banking` CPC ceiling of $211 is an anomaly.** Likely one large advertiser (Glia or Eltropy) bidding aggressively on very low volume. Set a bid cap of $40–$50 max for this keyword.

6. **Total addressable search volume is small (~5,000–6,000/mo across all qualified keywords).** This is consistent with CSXAI being in an emerging, pre-mass-awareness category. Google Ads will never be a high-volume channel for CSXAI — it's a high-intent, low-volume complement to LinkedIn outbound.
