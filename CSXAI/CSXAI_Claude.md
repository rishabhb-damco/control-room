CSX Intelligence is a voice agent platform that handles customer inquiries end-to-end for financial institutions and real estate companies. It operates 24/7, handles loan queries, compliance questions, and property inquiries — reducing human call-centre involvement. Key differentiators vs. competitors: empathy detection, compliance-native architecture, digital twin trained on each client's brand voice, deployment in weeks.
