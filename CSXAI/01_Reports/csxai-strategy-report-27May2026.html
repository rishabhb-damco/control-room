Why CSXAI wins here:
Not big enough for Glia's enterprise pricing, not small enough to use DIY tools. CSXAI's deployment speed + empathy positioning is built for this gap.
Credit Unions — High-Fit Segment
5,000+
NCUA-insured US credit unions. Member-first mandate aligns directly with CSXAI's empathy positioning
Decision-making advantage:
Smaller governance structure = faster buying decisions. C-suite is reachable directly on LinkedIn. No 18-month procurement cycle.
Real Estate Institutions — Secondary
~85K
Real estate brokerage firms in the US. Target: medium-large brokerages (50–1,000 employees) with inquiry call volume
⚠ Keyword trap identified:

Category Keywords — 3-Month Growth Trend
Paid Search Landscape is Open: Eltropy runs zero paid search. Glia runs minimal. The most-searched competitor brand in this category (1,900/mo) has no one defending it on Google. CSXAI can own this space from day one of campaign launch.
Conversational AI Banking is Exploding: Three keywords growing at +300% to +400% in 3 months. CPCs are near-zero because advertisers haven't noticed yet. First-mover advantage window is 60–90 days.
Total Search Volume is Small (~5,500/mo): This is a pre-mass-awareness category. Google will never be a high-volume channel for CSXAI — it's a high-intent complement to LinkedIn InMail outbound. Outbound creates the demand; Google captures the intent.
Meta Ads Not Recommended: Cannot reliably target VP Operations / CTO at a community bank on Facebook. Individual real estate agents (wrong ICP) are the only addressable audience. Budget is better deployed elsewhere.
