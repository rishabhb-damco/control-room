### Section A: The Key Competitors

|---|---|---|---|

---

### Section B: How They Generate Leads — Tactics Breakdown

**Glia's Lead Gen Machine (most relevant benchmark):**
- **#1 move: Industry report as lead magnet** — Published "2026 Banking AI Benchmarks Report" using data from 400 financial institutions. Covered in 10+ trade publications. Drives gated downloads and demo requests.
- **#2: "Request an Impact Study"** — Custom ROI forecast for each prospect. High-intent CTA that converts decision-makers.
- **#3: Annual event (Glia Interact)** — Generates pipeline, press, and customer retention in one event.
- **#4: Partner channel** — FIS integration partnership drives referral inbound.

**Eltropy's Lead Gen Machine:**
- **#1: SDR outbound + ABM** — Drove $6M → $33M pipeline in 6 months. Direct sales to named accounts.
- **#2: Webinars + community bank conferences** — Positions them as the "community bank expert."
- **#3: 50+ integration partnerships** — Every core banking system lists them → inbound referrals.

**What Both Do on LinkedIn:**
- Regular thought leadership from company page and leadership team
- Product news, customer wins, industry data posts
- Boosted posts (Thought Leader Ads format)

**What Neither Competitor Leads With:**
- Neither Eltropy nor Glia lead with **empathy** as a core message
- Both lead with **efficiency metrics** (handle 2x calls, 83% containment)
- **CSXAI's empathy + compliance positioning is currently unoccupied territory in this market**
