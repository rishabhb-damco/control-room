## Key Insights

### What's Working
- **Grief Search** — 11.35% CTR, $81 CPL, highest resonance of any campaign. Must relaunch in Q2 if counselor is available.
- **Phone call leads** — ~21% scheduling rate vs ~5% form fills. Single clearest ROI lever.
- **December** — $49 CPL when Grief + Generic ran together. This is the blueprint for Q2.
- **Advantage Plus Meta audience** — $26.61 CPL (best across all audiences). Emotion/mood targeting outperforms demographics.
- **Referral/internal** — 73.3% scheduling rate. PCP referral program has outsized potential ROI.
- **Funnel post-paperwork is strong** — 72.2% intake completion once paperwork is done. Bottleneck is pre-scheduling, not clinical.

### What's Not Working
- **Meta as cold-audience lead gen** — 59 leads, 0 scheduled. Medicaid/Medicare holders dominate Meta audience. Cannot fix this with targeting alone.
- **Demand Gen** — Google blocks Medication Management ads on YouTube without Healthcare certificate. $53 wasted.
- **CPL climbed $49 → $141 (Dec → Feb)** — Medicaid keyword exclusions are necessary but shrink the keyword pool. Scaling is hitting a ceiling.
- **GMB reviews** — Near-zero public reviews vs RCBM (2,273), Helios (205). Biggest trust gap.
- **Form-fill scheduling rate** — Too low (~5%). Over-reliance on forms hurts funnel.

### Opportunities
1. **DSA campaign** — Auto-capture long-tail queries outside policy restrictions
2. **Scale call-only campaigns + extensions** — 21% scheduling rate is the best digital signal
3. **Reactivate Grief Search** — If counselor capacity in Q2, this is the fastest CPL win
4. **Meta → Remarketing only** — Retarget website visitors + Google non-converters; stop cold-audience spend
5. **Google Healthcare certificate** — Unlocks Medication Management ads + YouTube
6. **Programmatic** — Michigan metro behavioral targeting; scale around Google restrictions
7. **25 Google Reviews in 90 days** — SMS/email post-appointment; RCBM's 4.7★ sets the bar
8. **Patient testimonials** — 5–10 consent-based; 20–40% CTR lift potential
9. **Medicare credentialing for 1 NP** — Opens senior segment all HIGH competitors share
10. **Insurance eligibility pre-qualifier on website** — Reduces ineligible leads entering funnel

### Watch List
- **Macomb Therapy Group** — Same building, accepts Medicaid/Medicare. Direct referral overflow.
- **BetterHelp** — Outspends 10:1 on ads. Organic + social needed as offset.
- **RCBM** — 4.7★ / 2,273 reviews sets a trust benchmark Wellspring is far below.
- **Telehealth (Cerebral, Done)** — Expanding insurance coverage. Track for Q3; structural threat.

## Learnings from Past Data

1. **Grief is the highest-intent keyword category** — 11.35% CTR proves it. Always run when counselor capacity exists.
2. **Phone calls convert 4x better than form fills** — 21% vs ~5%. Every campaign needs call extensions and call-only variants.
3. **Meta cannot replace Google for qualified lead gen** — Without hard insurance filters, Medicaid holders dominate. Conditional logic helps but is not a fix.
4. **December ($49 CPL) is the efficiency template** — Grief + Generic running together = optimal state.
5. **The funnel's biggest leak is Leads → Scheduled (82.1% drop)** — Primarily insurance mismatch + no-answer. Solve with pre-qualification form + automated follow-up.
6. **Advantage Plus emotional targeting beats demographic targeting** — $26.61 CPL vs $44–52 for demographic audiences.
7. **GLPG's 2.6★ GMB is an active conquest opportunity** — Run brand comparisons against them on Troy searches.
8. **Paperwork → Intake is strong (72.2%)** — Resources should focus on getting leads to schedule, not on clinical conversion.
9. **DSA launched post-QBR and is outperforming everything** — Consistently $22–$57 CPA vs Generic Search $62–$267. This is the most impactful structural change made in Q2.
10. **HAP insurance is a new blind spot** — Emerged in March 2026 as ~20-28% of lost leads. Was not in the QBR data. Need to confirm exclusion category and update all targeting and form logic.
11. **Psychotherapy is over-indexed** — 53–61% of inbound is psychotherapy queries. Medication Management is higher-value but under-captured. Ad copy specifically redirecting ADHD/ADD/Anxiety toward Med Mgmt is the lever.
12. **Scheduling rate is volatile week-to-week (20–50%)** — Not just a campaign quality issue. Provider capacity and form completion are the main variables. Need Wellspring-side alignment.
