Overview
What's Driving Performance

Before reviewing individual assets, here are the patterns consistently driving clicks and conversions across the account over this period. New copy should be built around these signals.

Location + Service Specificity

"Child & Teen Therapy Near You", "Mental Health Care in Troy MI" and "Best Therapist in Troy MI" all outperform generic alternatives — specificity filters intent.

Women-Specific Language

"Therapist for Women Near You" has the best CPL in the account at $19.48 with a 28.57% conversion rate — the strongest single signal in this data set.

Action-Oriented CTAs

"Schedule a Therapist Today" converts at 17.14%. Emotional copy alone ("Clarity Through Therapy", "Real Support, Real Progress") drives zero conversions.

"Feeling Heard" Emotional Hook

The top description ("Healing starts with feeling heard") drives the highest conversion volume — 10 conversions at $70.22 CPL. The emotional hook works in descriptions, not headlines.

Value / Budget Messaging

"Therapy That Fits Your Budget" converts at 12.50% — cost accessibility is a real purchase driver in the Michigan mental health market.

Grief Sitelinks Are Highly Efficient

The Grief Counseling sitelink has a 57.14% conversion rate and a $11.49 CPL — lowest in the account. Grief-adjacent intent is very high quality.
