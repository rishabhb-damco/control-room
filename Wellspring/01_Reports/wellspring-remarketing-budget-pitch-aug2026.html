A controlled remarketing test — using the audience you already have

Wellspring Therapeutic Partners · 12 August 2026

Opportunity→ Why remarketing→ Approach→ Budget→ Outcome→ Measurement

The opportunity

Remarketing pool size vs. Google's Search-remarketing threshold
  • ~400 users already sit in your Google Ads remarketing list — past Search/DSA visitors who didn't book.
  • Large enough for Display remarketing today; not yet large enough for Search remarketing (Google requires 1,000+).
  • These are warm visitors, not cold prospects — cheaper to bring back than to acquire fresh.

Why remarketing

  • Re-engages people who already found you and didn't book — for reasons already in your own data (insurance uncertainty, no-answer, incomplete forms).
  • Runs alongside Search/DSA, not instead of it: acquisition finds new demand, remarketing protects it.
  • Google is currently your only channel producing scheduled appointments — this extends that strength.

Proposed approach

  • Format: one Google Display remarketing campaign to your existing visitor list.
  • Message: insurance reassurance + 48-hour intake — deliberately different from acquisition ads.
  • Frequency: capped at 3–6 views/week per person — visible, not intrusive.
  • Window: 6-week test, matched to your weekly reporting cadence.

Expected outcome

Being direct: with 400 users, we're not forecasting a surge in bookings. Success here means proving the mechanics work — so we can scale with confidence once the pool passes 1,000.
