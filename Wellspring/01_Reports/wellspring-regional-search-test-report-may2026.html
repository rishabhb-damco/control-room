Section 4
Key Learnings
CTR of 17.08% is a strong signal. When the ad was shown, people clicked. This tells us the ad copy and targeting are relevant — the campaign is reaching the right people. This is not an awareness problem.
240 total impressions over 12 days is very low. The regional targeting is working but the search volume for therapy-specific, location-specific queries in Troy and Clinton Township is limited. We cannot scale this campaign on impression volume alone without expanding the keyword or geographic scope.
The landing page is not aligned with the ad intent. Visitors clicking on regional search ads — especially those searching for culturally specific therapy — land on a generic page. There is no message match, which explains the near-zero conversion rate.
The sole conversion came from "other" terms — not the tracked ones. This is an important signal: there may be effective queries being served that we cannot see because Google anonymises low-volume terms. These deserve more budget to surface and identify.
CPC is volatile ($3.76–$8.02). The campaign was still in the learning phase when it was paused. Google had not yet stabilised bidding, which explains the wide CPC swing. A longer run period or a target CPA bid strategy may have smoothed this out.
Section 5
What We Do Next
1
Build a faith-based therapy landing page

Create a dedicated page for Muslim therapist and culturally sensitive therapy queries. The page should acknowledge cultural background, mention faith-compatible therapy approaches, and include therapist profiles relevant to this audience. Until this page exists, rerunning the campaign will produce the same result.

2
Create a dedicated ad group for faith-based queries

Separate "muslim therapist", "faith-based therapist", "culturally sensitive therapy Troy" and related terms into their own ad group with tailored ad copy. This allows message match between the search, the ad, and the landing page — the conversion funnel will close.

3
Investigate the "other search terms" conversion

The one conversion came from a query Google anonymised. Look in the Google Ads UI search terms report filtered to this campaign to identify what it was. If it is a recurring query, add it as an exact match keyword and build around it.

4
Expand geographic targeting and keyword scope

240 impressions over 12 days is too low to draw strong conclusions. Broaden the keyword list to include suburb-level therapy searches (Sterling Heights, Auburn Hills, Rochester Hills) and test broader match types to understand the full demand landscape before narrowing down.

5
Relaunch only after landing page is ready

Do not resume the campaign on the generic Wellspring homepage. The ad relevance is proven (17% CTR). The only missing piece is a landing page that converts. Relaunch with the new page, set a 30-day test window, and track form submissions and call clicks separately for this campaign.

Section 6
Test Verdict
⚠

Inconclusive — Promising signal, broken funnel

The Regional Search campaign proved that demand exists and that the ads are relevant (17.08% CTR). However, the conversion funnel was not ready — the landing page did not match the intent of the searches being served, particularly the faith-based therapy queries. The $211 spent returned 1 conversion at $211/lead, which is not viable. This is not a failure of the campaign concept — it is a setup gap. The test should be re-run once a dedicated landing page is built and the faith-based ad group is isolated properly.
