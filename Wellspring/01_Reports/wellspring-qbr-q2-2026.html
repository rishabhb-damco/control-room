Act III — Channel Performance
Search Is Changing —
We're Ahead of It
Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Perplexity are increasingly answering healthcare searches directly, before a user clicks any result. For mental health specifically, people are asking questions like "What's the best therapist near me that takes BCBS?" and receiving an AI-generated answer.
Wellspring is already being cited. Semrush confirms 21 pages are cited across ChatGPT, AI Overview/Mode, and Gemini — with an AI Visibility Score of 14. We went from 0 AI sessions in Q1 to 5 in Q2. This is early but real signal. Most local competitors aren’t on the board yet.
The gap is AI Mode. We have 0 citations in AI Mode today. Q3 priority: ship every service page with FAQPage schema and a citable action sentence so AI engines can surface Wellspring as the answer — target: 30+ citations, 5+ AI Mode entries by Sep 30.

Act III — Channel Performance
Instagram — The
Foundation We Laid
What We're Saying Up Front
"Instagram launched in late June. There is no Q2 performance data to report — and that is entirely by design."
Building brand recognition before a campaign is measured is a deliberate choice. Instagram's role is to plant the Wellspring name before someone feels the need for therapy. That awareness doesn't convert in week one — it shows up as better paid search performance six to twelve weeks later.
Why we started it now: Google Ads captures people who are already searching. Instagram reaches people before they search. As paid search competition increases and CPL trends upward, the only durable defence is upstream brand awareness. We're building that now.
What We Set Up in Q2
✅
Account created and branded
Profile, bio, link-in-bio, and highlight covers aligned with Wellspring's visual identity.
✅
Content pillars defined
Three themes: Mental health education, Practice culture, and Patient journey stories.
✅
Posting cadence established
3× per week target for Q3. Q2 was setup and initial posts only.
What to Watch in Q3
Profile visits, reach growth, and story views are the leading indicators. We'll review at 90 days — by end of Q3 Instagram should be contributing to brand search volume.

Act V — Q3 Strategy
What We’re Doing in Q3
01
Restructure paid campaigns: DSA-first
Shift budget split from 35/65 (DSA/Generic) to 70/30. Resolve delivery conflict permanently. Pause Generic Search keywords where DSA already covers the intent. Expected CPL reduction: ~20%.
Channel: Google Ads  ·  Timeline: July 2026
02
Launch Google Display remarketing
Target visitors who landed on the site but didn't convert. Retargeted leads convert at 40–60% lower CPL than cold clicks. Small incremental budget with high expected efficiency.
Channel: Google Ads  ·  Timeline: Aug 2026
03
Scale Instagram to 3×/week posting
Establish consistent content cadence: 1 educational post, 1 practice culture post, 1 therapy insight per week. 90-day review in October. Instagram is a long-game channel — consistency now pays in Q4.
Channel: Instagram  ·  Timeline: Ongoing from July
04
Insurance-specific SEO & AEO content
Build dedicated pages for "Does Wellspring accept [Insurance]?" — high-intent queries with zero competition. Positions Wellspring for AI Overview citations and direct insurance-motivated searches.
Channel: SEO  ·  Timeline: Aug–Sep 2026

What We Need
from You to Make Q3 Work
1
HAP insurance decision — accept or definitively close
This is the single highest-impact action available to increase lead conversion in Q3. An estimated 8–12 additional bookings per month depend on this decision. If HAP stays unresolved, we will redirect ads away from audiences likely to carry HAP to reduce wasted spend.
Critical — needed by Jul 2026
2
Weekly capacity — how many new patients can you onboard?
Our lead volume target depends on knowing the ceiling. If capacity is 8 new patients per week, we optimise for 10–12 leads. If capacity has increased, we scale accordingly. A number from you each month lets us calibrate spend correctly.
High priority — monthly update
3
Instagram content — 2–3 original photos or videos per week
Stock photography does not work for a therapy practice. Patients need to see real people and real spaces to feel safe enough to reach out. The most effective Instagram content for a mental health practice is authentic and low-production — a photo of the office, a short therapist introduction, a real quote.
Ongoing from July
4
Google Healthcare certificate — unlock YouTube & Medication Management ads
YouTube pre-roll and expanded Medication Management targeting are currently blocked until the certification is completed. We can guide the process — Wellspring simply needs to initiate the application. Completing this unlocks a significant new acquisition channel at no additional cost.
High priority — Q3
