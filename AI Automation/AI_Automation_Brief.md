## Goal

> Stop spending time on execution. Come in, think creatively, and let the tools do the rest.

**Framework:**
1. Every person lists all tasks they do — daily, weekly, monthly
2. Estimate time per task — quick / medium / long
3. Identify the bottom 3 (longest, most repetitive) per person
4. Build Claude skills or routines to handle them
5. Start with 3–4 skills per department, then expand
